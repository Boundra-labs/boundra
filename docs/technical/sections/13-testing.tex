\section{Benchmark: B0–B3}

The evaluation runner compares four ways of reaching a mandate. All use the same mandate builder and action set; only elicitation and consent differ.

\begin{stable}{@{}P{1.2cm}P{6.6cm}L@{}}
\headrow \thead{Method} & \thead{Elicitation} & \thead{Consent}\\ \midrule
B0 & One model call reads preferences from the goal text & The draft is the mandate\\
B1 & Same one-shot draft & Static confirmation screen\\
B2 & Five fixed questions: liquidity, lockup, leverage, amount, vaults & Same approval step as the product\\
B3 & ACCE adaptive questions & Same approval step as the product\\
\end{stable}

A \emph{mismatch} is a mandate that breaks at least one of the persona's non-negotiable preferences. The fixture has 50 personas (20 development, 30 held out). Hidden preferences are read only by the simulator, a lint rule stops baselines from importing it, and the held-out split refuses to run until the fixture is frozen.
