\chapter{Limitations and roadmap}\label{ch:roadmap}

\section{Known limitations}

\begin{stable}{@{}P{4.4cm}L@{}}
\headrow \thead{Limitation} & \thead{Why, and what it means}\\ \midrule
Testnet only & Base Sepolia, MockUSDC and two controlled mock vaults. Illustrative yields do not accrue; principal stays 1:1.\\
Small action set & Two vaults and one deposit allocation per mandate. Deliberately narrow, so the guarantees are easy to check.\\
Risk events are a fixture & A risk admin sets vault risk by hand to stand in for a real incident; there is no oracle.\\
No fee replacement & A stuck transaction is reconciled or released after its deadline; fees are never bumped automatically.\\
Benchmark not yet run & The persona fixture awaits label review and freeze; no results are claimed.\\
Packages not published & The SDK and MCP server are built and tested but not on npm; publishing needs a licence decision.\\
Free hosting sleeps & On Render's free plan the agent sleeps with the service when nobody visits.\\
\end{stable}

\begin{principle}{Before any real funds}
An independent audit of the contracts, integrations with real, reviewed vaults, a review of the agent's policy for real market conditions, and a legal review. Until then, Boundra stays on testnet.
\end{principle}
